Rules

What keeps the three options one family.

  • One theme. Every page is ink. Blueprint blue appears as panels and controls, never as a page background.
  • Radius 0 on every element. The app-icon tile is the one rounded shape, and it is a brand asset.
  • Mono is semantic: commands, paths, versions, table headers, the wordmark. Body and headings are grotesk.
  • At most one small uppercase label per three sections. Headlines stand alone.
  • No em-dashes anywhere on the site. Sentences split with a period or a colon.
  • Hairlines organize content or separate sections. None are drawn for atmosphere.
  • The construction drawing is the only illustration. No fake terminals, no div screenshots.
  • Spacing on a 4px base: 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128. Section gap 96 on landing, 48 in docs.
  • Type scale: 56 / 40 / 32 / 22 / 17 / 14 / 13 (code) / 12 (labels).
  • Layout: 1440 max, 64px page gutters. Docs: 264 sidebar, 760 article, 200 outline.
