# Signals and thresholds

The signals `stackgen-reputation` reads, the ecosystems each applies to, the
source it comes from (by the short name `sources.md` uses), and the two
thresholds — `block` and `warn` — with one line of why. A number here is a
written-down default, not a judgement made at run time; change the table,
not the run.

**Decision rule.** Any `block` signal blocks; otherwise any `warn` signal
warns; otherwise pass. A cell reading `unavailable` means no source in
`sources.md` carries that signal for that ecosystem, so it contributes
nothing to the verdict and is not listed as fired — it is never inferred
from another ecosystem's data or from recollection.

**The version.** Every version-scoped row runs against the one version the
skill resolved — the argument's, or the registry's default — and the row's
`name` column shows which. Ages are measured from the `checked_at` of the
run, in days.

## Packages — `npm`, `pypi`, `pub`

| Signal                  | Ecosystems         | Source                                                   | `block`                                                                                                            | `warn`                                                           | Why                                                                                                                                     |
| ----------------------- | ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| exists in registry      | npm, pypi, pub     | `depsdev` (npm, pypi); `pubdev` score endpoint (pub)     | the registry answers 404, or the resolved version is not among the package's versions                              | —                                                                | a name nothing serves is a typo or a squat waiting to be registered; either way nothing to pin                                           |
| first-publish age       | npm, pypi          | `depsdev` — earliest `published_at` across `versions`    | under 30 days                                                                                                      | —                                                                | a package younger than a month has no track record; the classic squat window                                                            |
| first-publish age       | pub                | unavailable                                              | —                                                                                                                  | —                                                                | pub.dev's verified endpoints carry no publish dates                                                                                     |
| latest-publish age      | npm, pypi          | `depsdev` — latest `published_at` across `versions`      | —                                                                                                                  | over 730 days                                                    | two years without a release is abandonment more often than completion                                                                   |
| latest-publish age      | pub                | unavailable                                              | —                                                                                                                  | —                                                                | as above                                                                                                                                |
| downloads               | npm                | `npm-downloads` — `last-week`, daily entries summed      | —                                                                                                                  | under 1,000 in the week                                          | the bottom tier: nobody else is running it, so nobody else is finding its faults                                                        |
| downloads               | pub                | `pubdev` — `downloadCount30Days`                         | —                                                                                                                  | under 1,000 in the 30 days                                       | as above, on the window pub.dev publishes                                                                                               |
| downloads               | pypi               | unavailable                                              | —                                                                                                                  | —                                                                | PyPI's JSON API reports `-1` for every download count; no verified source carries the number                                             |
| maintainer count        | npm                | `npm-registry` — length of `maintainers`                 | —                                                                                                                  | exactly one                                                      | a single maintainer is a single account to phish, and a single life event away from abandonment                                         |
| maintainer count        | pypi               | `pypi` — `ownership.roles`, where the response carries it | —                                                                                                                  | exactly one role listed                                          | as above; silent when the block is absent, which the verified doc allows                                                                |
| verified publisher      | pub                | `pubdev` — `publisherId`                                 | —                                                                                                                  | `null`                                                           | an unverified publisher is an individual account with no domain behind it                                                               |
| deprecated              | npm, pypi          | `depsdev` — `is_deprecated` on the resolved version      | `true`                                                                                                             | —                                                                | the registry's own word that this is not to be used                                                                                     |
| yanked                  | pypi               | `pypi` — `info.yanked` on the resolved release           | `true`                                                                                                             | —                                                                | a yanked release is withdrawn by its author; resolvers skip it and so should a pin                                                      |
| deprecated              | pub                | unavailable                                              | —                                                                                                                  | —                                                                | pub.dev's discontinued flag is not on a verified endpoint                                                                               |
| advisories on version   | npm, pypi          | `depsdev` — `advisory_keys` on the resolved version, then the advisory endpoint for each id's `cvss3_score`; `osv` by id for the `fixed` event and the severity word | any advisory with `cvss3_score` of 7.0 or above, or, where deps.dev has no score for it, `ecosystem_specific.severity` reading `HIGH` or `CRITICAL` | any advisory below that line, or one carrying no severity from either source | deps.dev lists only the advisories that affect the version asked about, so a hit is by definition unpatched at that version. An id with no `cvss3_score` is decided by OSV's word alone, so OSV unreachable there is the row `UNRESOLVED`, per `sources.md` |
| advisories on version   | pub                | unavailable                                              | —                                                                                                                  | —                                                                | deps.dev does not index pub, and OSV's package query is a POST `WebFetch` cannot send                                                     |
| provenance, attestation | npm, pypi          | `depsdev` — `slsa_provenances`, `attestations`           | —                                                                                                                  | both empty                                                       | no verifiable link from the artifact to a build of the source; the registry account is the whole chain of custody                       |
| provenance, attestation | pub                | unavailable                                              | —                                                                                                                  | —                                                                | not on a verified endpoint                                                                                                              |
| OpenSSF Scorecard       | npm, pypi          | `depsdev` — `scorecard.overall_score` of the source project | —                                                                                                                  | below 4.0                                                        | the aggregate of branch protection, review, CI tests, dependency pinning; low is a project that takes none of the usual precautions      |
| OpenSSF Scorecard       | pub                | unavailable                                              | —                                                                                                                  | —                                                                | deps.dev does not index pub                                                                                                             |
| near-name               | npm, pub           | `npm-downloads` / `pubdev` for the candidate's downloads | edit distance of 2 or less to a package with at least 100× this one's downloads, both counts fetched               | —                                                                | a squat is a popular name misspelled; the download ratio is what separates the squat from the original                                  |
| near-name               | pypi               | unavailable                                              | —                                                                                                                  | —                                                                | needs downloads on both sides, which no verified source carries for PyPI                                                                |

**Near-name candidates.** The skill holds no list of popular packages, and
`sources.md` verifies no search endpoint. The candidate set is therefore the
model's own recall of well-known names within distance 2 of the one given
— and every candidate is then **fetched**: it exists on the registry, and its
downloads are read from the same endpoint as the name under test. A
candidate that does not resolve, or whose count is under the 100× line, is
dropped. The recall proposes; the fetch decides. Nothing about the candidate
is asserted from memory.

## Actions — `action:` (owner/repo, optional `@ref`)

| Signal                  | Source                                                                           | `block`                                                            | `warn`                                                           | Why                                                                                                                       |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| repository exists       | `github` — repository endpoint                                                   | 404                                                                | —                                                                | an action is its repository; no repository, no action                                                                     |
| repository archived     | `github` — `archived`, `disabled`                                                | either `true`                                                      | —                                                                | archived is the owner's word that it is done; disabled is GitHub's                                                        |
| repository age          | `github` — `created_at`                                                          | under 30 days                                                      | —                                                                | same window as a package's first publish, for the same reason                                                             |
| latest release age      | `github` — latest-release endpoint, `published_at`                               | —                                                                  | over 730 days, or 404 (no release at all)                        | an action with no release is pinned by branch or bare tag; one two years stale is unmaintained against a moving runner    |
| ref resolves            | `github` — git-ref endpoint, for a tag ref                                       | 404                                                                | —                                                                | the pinned tag does not exist; a workflow would fail at first run, or worse resolve to whatever gets that name next        |
| ref is a tag, not a SHA | syntactic — the ref given: a 40-hex string is a SHA, anything else is a tag      | —                                                                  | a tag, or no ref                                                 | a tag can be moved; a SHA cannot. The table says which the caller chose; the caller's convention decides whether it minds |
| OpenSSF Scorecard       | `depsdev` — project endpoint for `github.com/<owner>/<repo>`, `scorecard.overall_score` | —                                                                  | below 4.0                                                        | as for packages                                                                                                           |
| stars                   | `github` — `stargazers_count`                                                    | —                                                                  | under 50                                                         | a rough proxy for how many eyes have been on it; low is not damning, which is why it warns                                |
| advisories              | unavailable                                                                      | —                                                                  | —                                                                | OSV's query by repository is a POST `WebFetch` cannot send, and deps.dev's advisory list is per package version, not per repo |

## Images — `image:` (registry/repo, optional `:tag`)

| Signal        | Source                                                       | `block`                            | `warn`                                 | Why                                                                                                       |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| tag exists (`docker.io`) | `dockerhub` — the tag listed under `results[].name`, any page | repository 404, or the tag on no page | —                                  | the image, at that tag, is not on Docker Hub                                                             |
| tag exists (other hosts) | unavailable                                              | —                                  | —                                      | the only registry-neutral check is an OCI `HEAD` behind a bearer token, which `WebFetch` cannot send; `ghcr.io` has no GET-able alternative |
| mutable tag   | syntactic — the tag given                                    | —                                  | `latest`, or no tag                    | `latest` and an untagged pull move under the pin; a digest or a version tag is the reproducible spelling |
| last push age | unavailable                                                  | —                                  | —                                      | Docker Hub's per-tag push timestamp is not on a verified endpoint; GHCR has no such API at all             |
| advisories    | unavailable                                                  | —                                  | —                                      | scanning an image's contents is outside a read of its manifest                                            |
